# RAL-270 — Main Dashboard as a landing target — and what office users get instead

**V18-28** — plan: `docs/v1.8/Phase_Plan.md` §3.5. Depends on V18-20 ([RAL-262](https://linear.app/ralphoksiprojects/issue/RAL-262/permission-aware-landing-selector-user-form-division-config-office)).

Main Dashboard is the first of the three landing targets. It is straightforward for PPDO users and needs a decision for office users.

**The constraint: **`(portal)/layout.tsx:208-217` bounces office users out of everything outside Budget Planning (plus `/account`). So Main Dashboard is currently **unreachable** for them, and offering it as a landing page would produce a redirect ping-pong.

Two options:

* **Don't offer it to office users** — the options list filters it out. *Recommended:* the gate exists for a reason, and the Main Dashboard's content (PPDO calendar, PPDO stats, resource links) is not theirs.
* **Relax the gate** so office users can reach it. Bigger change with its own scoping questions (whose events? whose stats?), and it partly re-opens what [RAL-230](https://linear.app/ralphoksiprojects/issue/RAL-230/stop-office-users-fetching-the-ppdo-scoped-budget-planning-dashboard) deliberately closed.

**Done when:** Main Dashboard is selectable by users who can reach it, is not selectable by users who cannot, and no combination of preference + gate produces a loop.
