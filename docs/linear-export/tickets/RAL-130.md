# RAL-130 — Config: audit Chart of Accounts + reseed reserve eligibility, semi-expendable + FE accounts (Q13)

> 🌿 **Working branch: **`release/1.4.0`. PR targets it, **not **`main`. Child of epic [RAL-116](https://linear.app/ralphoksiprojects/issue/RAL-116/wfp-rework-epic-v14).

## Why

The accounts config seed needs a correctness pass before real WFP entry relies on it. Four issues, all found while reviewing the current export (`20260710_103644_accounts.csv`) against the WFP form's **"Accounts with Reserve"** sheet, **annex B** (Local Government Chart of Accounts), and **COA Circular 2024-006** (semi-expendable items):

1. **Reserve eligibility is mis-seeded.** ⚠️ The "10% reserve" flag Ralph asked for **already exists** — it's `default_apply_reserve` (added by [RAL-117](https://linear.app/ralphoksiprojects/issue/RAL-117/config-chart-of-accounts-rework-expense-class-default-nature-default)). **Do NOT add a new column.** This ticket corrects its *values*: set `default_apply_reserve = true` for exactly the **42 MOOE accounts** in the "Accounts with Reserve" sheet, `false` everywhere else. The current CSV has it wrong (e.g. a PS "Salaries and Wages - Casual/Contractual" row marked `true`; most genuinely reserve-eligible MOOE rows marked `false`).
2. **4 semi-expendable accounts are MISSING** from the CSV but present in "Accounts with Reserve": `5-02-03-210` (Semi-Expendable Machinery and Equipment Expense), `5-02-03-220` (Semi-Expendable Books, Furnitures and Fixtures), `5-02-13-210` and `5-02-13-220` (their Repairs & Maintenance counterparts). Source titles/numbers from **COA Circular 2024-006** + annex B.
3. **Q13 — FE classification.** The 6 Financial-Expense accounts (`5-03-01-xxx`: Management Supervision/Trusteeship Fees, Interest, Guarantee Fees, Bank Charges, Commitment Fees, Other Financial Charges) are currently `expense_class = CO` in the CSV — wrong. This is the open **Q13** from the tracker. ⚠️ **Confirm with Ralph:** make `FE` a real 4th `expense_class` (requires adding it to the config-page dropdown, currently PS/MOOE/CO/Other only, + `AccountService` validation) vs. reclassify as `Other` vs. exclude from WFP.
4. **Test row **`9-99-99-999` ("Test [RAL-117](https://linear.app/ralphoksiprojects/issue/RAL-117/config-chart-of-accounts-rework-expense-class-default-nature-default) Account") must be dropped from the real seed.

Full-list audit: reconcile the entire `5-*` expense series against annex B and add any other missing accounts.
