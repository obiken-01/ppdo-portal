# RAL-264 — PWA: point start_url at a neutral resolver instead of /dashboard

**V18-19** — plan: `docs/v1.8/Phase_Plan.md` §3.3. Follows V18-18.

`frontend/src/app/manifest.ts:36` sets `start_url: "/dashboard"`. A web-app manifest is **one static document served to everyone** — it cannot vary per user. So for anyone who installs the portal ([RAL-234](https://linear.app/ralphoksiprojects/issue/RAL-234/pwa-groundwork-offline-honest-reconnecting-shared-app-version-swa) shipped PWA Phase 1), the icon on their home screen ignores their landing preference entirely.

Fix: point `start_url` at a neutral resolver — `/` or a small `/home` route — that redirects through `resolveLandingPath` once the user is known.

Small change, easy to forget, and it silently breaks the whole feature for exactly the users most likely to notice (the ones who installed the app). Check the service-worker's cached navigation fallback at the same time, so an installed client doesn't serve a stale `/dashboard` shell offline.

**Done when:** launching the installed app lands on the user's resolved landing page, and the offline shell still works.
