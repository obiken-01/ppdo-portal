# RAL-267 — Tell the user their password was reset, and when

**V18-27** — plan: `docs/v1.8/Phase_Plan.md` §3.4 — see the **V18-27** row of that table.

> ⚠️ **Corrected 2026-08-26.** This ticket previously cited "sub-decision (k) in `AIP_Requirements_Review.md` §2.6". **That document does not exist in the repo and never has** — do not go looking for it. `docs/v1.8/Phase_Plan.md` §3.4 is the authoritative source.

Depends on V18-22 ([RAL-253](https://linear.app/ralphoksiprojects/issue/RAL-253/password-reset-schema-for-recovery-answer-mustchangepassword-attempt)).

Show a notice at next login: *"Your password was reset on {date}. If this wasn't you, contact the administrator."*

**Why this is not cosmetic.** Option B is honestly weak against a *colleague* — someone in the same office may well know the answer to "what town were you born in". What makes it acceptable anyway is that a reset **cannot happen unnoticed**: the real user sees it the next time they log in, and V18-07's audit row says when and from where. Without this notice, a colleague-initiated takeover leaves the victim with nothing but a mysteriously changed password.

Small: a timestamp column (or read the audit log), a dismissible banner, cleared on acknowledgement.

**Done when:** any reset — self-service or admin-initiated — surfaces to the account owner on their next login.
