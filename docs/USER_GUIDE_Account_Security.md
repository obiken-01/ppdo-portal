# PPDO Portal — Your Account and Password

A short guide to signing in, setting up account recovery, and getting back in if you forget
your password.

Written for everyone who uses the Portal. You do not need any technical background to follow it.

If you get stuck at any point, contact the **PPDO Portal Site Admin**. Never share your password
or your security answer with anyone — including staff who are helping you.

---

## 1. The one-time security question

The first time you sign in after the account-recovery update, the Portal shows a single screen
titled **Set up account recovery** before you reach your usual page.

This appears for **every account**, including accounts that have existed for years. If you see it,
nothing is wrong with your account and nothing has been changed — it is a one-time step for
everyone.

**Your password is not changing.** You will keep signing in exactly as you do today. The security
question is separate. It exists so that if you ever forget your password, you can prove the account
is yours and get back in without waiting for an administrator.

You cannot skip this screen. It takes about a minute, and you will not be asked again.

### Choosing your question

You choose one of five questions:

- What town or municipality were you born in?
- What is your mother's maiden name?
- What was the name of your elementary school?
- What was the name of your first pet?
- What is the first name of your childhood best friend?

The list is fixed — you cannot write your own question. This is deliberate: people who write their
own often choose something they cannot answer the same way later.

### Choosing your answer

- **Pick something that will not change.** You may not need this for a year or more. A favourite
  or a current fact is a poor choice; a fact about your past is a good one.
- **Capitalisation and spaces do not matter.** `Manila`, `manila`, and `manila ` are all treated as
  the same answer. You will not be locked out by how you typed it.
- **Keep the answer private.** Anyone who knows it can begin a password reset on your account.
  Treat it like your password.
- **Short answers are fine.** One word is usually best — the less room for you to phrase it
  differently later, the better.

## 2. If you forget your password

On the sign-in page, select **Forgot password?**. The Portal will take you through three steps.

**Step 1 — Enter your username.**
The Portal shows you the security question saved on your account.

**Step 2 — Answer your security question.**
Type the answer you saved earlier. Capitalisation and spaces do not matter.

**Step 3 — Copy your temporary password.**
If your answer is correct, the Portal displays a **temporary password** on screen, with a button to
copy it.

> ⚠️ **This temporary password is shown once and cannot be shown again.** Copy it before closing
> the window. If you lose it, simply start **Forgot password?** again.

Now sign in with your username and that temporary password. The Portal will immediately ask you to
choose a new password of your own. Once you have set it, you are back in as normal.

### If it does not work

For your protection, the Portal gives the same general message whether the username was not
recognised, the answer was wrong, or no security question has been set up yet. It will not confirm
which. Check your username first, then your answer. If neither works, contact the Site Admin.

---

## 3. If you answer incorrectly too many times

You may attempt your security question **five times per hour**. After a fifth incorrect answer, the
Portal stops accepting attempts on that account for the remainder of the hour, counted from your
first incorrect answer.

This protects your account from someone guessing at your answer.

If you are locked out, you have two options:

- **Wait, then try again.** The limit clears on its own within the hour.
- **Contact the Site Admin**, who can reset your password directly (see below). You do not have to
  wait for the hour to pass if you need access sooner.

---

## 4. If an administrator resets your password for you

An administrator can reset your password without your security question — for example if you are
locked out and cannot wait.

When they do, the Portal generates a **one-time password** and shows it to the administrator on
screen. It is never stored and never sent by email or message, so the administrator must pass it to
you directly. If it is lost before it reaches you, they simply reset it again.

When you sign in with that one-time password, the Portal requires you to choose your own password
before you can continue. The administrator does not know the password you choose.

---

## 5. "Your password was reset" notice

After any reset, the Portal shows you a notice telling you that your password was reset and when.

**If you did not expect this notice, tell the Site Admin promptly.** It means a reset was performed
on your account that you did not request.

---

## 6. Good habits

- Do not share your password or security answer with anyone, including staff assisting you. Nobody
  from PPDO will ever ask you for either.
- Do not reuse your Portal password on other websites.
- If you think someone else knows your password, change it — you can do this at any time from the
  **Account** page, under Security.
- Sign out when you are finished on a shared computer.

---

## Getting help

Contact the **PPDO Portal Site Admin** for anything to do with your account: sign-in problems,
lockouts, a forgotten security answer, or a reset notice you did not expect.
